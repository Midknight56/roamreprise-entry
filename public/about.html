ROOM FOR A CHANGE OF PLANS

How this works.

Pick what you enjoy. Get two days in Lisbon. See how a failed reservation changes the route, the schedule and the money.

  1. Review a plan and approve its quote.
  2. Authorize test money with a sandbox card or PayPal wallet.
  3. Hold bookings with the Simulated supplier.
  4. Make the hotel unavailable. Compare checked replacements and their schedule changes.
  5. Accept a repair. Void the old hold and approve a new one.
  6. Confirm the bookings, then capture the payment.
  7. A supplier cancels an activity. The slot is replanned and the price difference is refunded.

Who it is for.

Picture a weekend in Lisbon booked from five places: a hotel, two airport cars, a tile painting class and a fado dinner. Two days before you fly, the hotel cancels. The cars were timed to that hotel and the class was picked because it was a short walk away. The card was already charged for pieces that only made sense together.

RoamReprise is for travelers who book one trip across several suppliers, and for the small tour operator who sells that trip as one package. When one supplier fails, the plan is rebuilt with every check shown, and the money follows: an old hold is released, a new total needs a fresh approval, and a cancelled activity after payment becomes a refund.

Real places. Simulated bookings.

Hotels, meals, activities and cars use local fixtures labelled “Simulated supplier.” No real travel is purchased. A confirmed booking here is a simulation.

Sandbox payments.

The live app sends authorization, void, capture and refund requests to PayPal sandbox. All money is test money. The server sets amounts from the approved quote. A changed plan needs new approval.

A lost response means “Checking payment.” Further payment writes wait while the server reads PayPal's state. A refund stays pending until PayPal reports completion.

The URL option ?mock=1 uses browser data and makes no PayPal or AI calls. Mock ids start with MOCK-. Recorded runs show saved evidence with a capture date and send no PayPal requests.

AI within a daily cap.

Workers AI can pick offers, a checked repair and changes you request before or during a hold, before capture. Code checks prices, times, availability and travel buffers. AI never sets payment fields.

At most 4 AI calls run per trip: one plan, one repair and up to two changes you ask for. Each reserves 15 neurons before it starts. This app's cap is 900 neurons per UTC day, allowing at most 60 such reservations. A shared account guard stops reservations at 9,500 neurons. The budget ledger must approve every call.

If the cap is used up, the ledger is unavailable, or the model gives invalid ids, the app uses a rule choice or a recorded answer for the default trip. The notebook labels each step as AI picked · code checked, AI pick (cached today, no new call), Recorded AI answer or Rule pick. Cached answers last 24 hours. Changes you request use a live answer or the rules, with no cached answer.

Tools behind the trip.

Plain JavaScript modules run the app. Three.js draws the dotted globe. Natural Earth land polygons are public domain. An original canvas sketch draws Lisbon routes.

The route sketch is drawn by the app; no map tiles are used.

Bryntum Scheduler shows two days, travel buffers and dependencies. Its trial watermark stays visible. A basic timeline works if the Scheduler cannot load. Cloudflare Workers, Durable Objects and D1 handle the server, trip state and shared usage ledger. PayPal sandbox handles test payments.
